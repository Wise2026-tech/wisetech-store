Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

WISETECH respects customer privacy and collects only information reasonably required to process orders, provide support, and operate the website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the service, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Selected product or subscription
  • Order and payment references
  • Service-specific identifiers required for fulfillment

2. Payment Information

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers such as Paystack. WISETECH does not need to store your full card details or mobile-money PIN.

3. How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Verify payment status
  • Send order confirmation and status emails
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain transaction records
  • Prevent fraud and misuse

4. Third-Party Services

WISETECH may rely on providers such as Paystack, Render, email providers, telecommunications providers, and subscription-service partners. Information is shared only where reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

5. Account Passwords

Customers should not send permanent account passwords to WISETECH unless a legitimate service process specifically requires temporary access. WISETECH does not intend to store customer streaming-service passwords.

6. Data Retention

Order records may be retained for business, accounting, customer-support, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes.

7. Security

WISETECH uses reasonable security measures including server-side payment verification, protected administrative access, and restricted access to operational data.

8. Customer Requests

Customers may contact WISETECH with reasonable privacy questions or requests concerning their personal information, subject to legal and record-keeping requirements.

9. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the business, website, or service providers change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the details on our Contact & Support page.
